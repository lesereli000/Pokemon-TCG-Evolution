# S1G2-Project
Constraints of Pokemon Trading Card Game:

Set-up:

1. Players

a. There are two players, who both have one deck

2. Deck

a. 60 cards

b. Maximum of four copies of any card

i. EXCEPT basic energy card


Gameplay:

1. Flip coin. Winner decides which player goes first

2. Shuffle your 60-card deck

3. Draw top 7 cards

4. If you have ONE OR MORE basic Pokémon in your hand, put ONE down as your active Pokémon

5. If you have more than ONE basic Pokémon in your hand, put up to 5 basic pokemon on your bench.
